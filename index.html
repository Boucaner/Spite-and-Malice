How to Play

Setup

Two standard decks are shuffled together. Each player gets a 20-card goal pile, dealt face-down with the top card flipped face-up. Whoever shows the highest card goes first.

Your Turn

Draw from the stock until you're holding 5 cards, play as many cards as you can to the center stacks, then finish by discarding one card from your hand onto one of your four side stacks.

Center Stacks

Up to four center stacks can be active at once. A stack can only be started with an Ace, then must be built in order — A, 2, 3, ... up to Queen. A completed 12-card stack is cleared away and can return to the draw pile later.

What You Can Play

On your turn you can play the top card of your goal pile, the top card of any of your side stacks, or any card from your hand — onto a legal center stack.

Kings Are Wild

Kings can be played on any center stack and stand in for whatever card the sequence needs next.

Side Stacks

You can build up to four side stacks by discarding one card onto them at the end of your turn. Any card can go on top regardless of rank — but only the top card of each stack is playable.

How to Win

Be the first to clear your entire 20-card goal pile.
